01

Overview

What Celestial Store Space does

The central tool Xfinity Retail Store reps use to run the floor. From Store Space you can:

  • Manage appointments and the check-in queue.
  • Pack Buy Anywhere Pickup in Store (BAPIS) orders.
  • Complete Point of Sale purchases.
  • Open Celestial Customer Space for member account handling.

What this job aid covers

Three parts. A and B follow a guest through the store; C covers member accounts in Customer Space.

Part A · Sections 2–6 Celestial Store Space

Queue screens, checking in members, prospects and Comcast Business customers, creating appointments, reading customer details and AI insights, and starting, ending or transferring a session.

 Part B · Sections 7–15 Celestial Point of Sale

The ground rules, launching POS and Back Office, the POS homepage, step-by-step sales and returns, opening, funding, settling and closing the cash drawer, and where to get help.

 Part C · Sections 16–23 Celestial Customer Space

The Retail 360 replacement: account lookup, new-sale and existing-member paths, billing, services and devices, Mercury Assistant, trainings and FAQs.
POS scope — read this first The Celestial POS should be used to complete sales and returns for in-store merchandise and bill payments for customers. It should not be used to sell Xfinity services or equipment that needs activation.
Part A

Celestial Store Space

Run the guest queue: check people in, book appointments, prepare from their details, and hand off to the right tool.

02

Queue (Home) Screen

Getting started

Select Store in the side bar to open your store's queue. This is the home screen for everything in Part A.

  • Navigation bar — Store opens the retail store queue. The other icons take you to apps such as Einstein 360 or ECM, and to the Celestial home page for Knowledge.
  • Store information — The drop-down arrow next to Store shows which store's queue you're viewing. Switch between Guests (the main queue) and Calendar (all upcoming appointments, today and future).
  • Start an action — The person icon starts a check-in (section 3). The calendar icon creates an appointment (section 4). Both icons sit at the top of every Store Space page.
  • Status columns
    • Appointments — every customer with an appointment scheduled for today.
    • Waiting — guests who are checked in and waiting, with how long they've waited.
    • In Progress — guests you're helping, plus guests other reps are helping and which rep has them.
    Select the list icon beside a column to open that column's full screen.

Appointments screen

All of today's upcoming appointments, whether each guest is a new or existing customer, and a summary of the visit reason. Select a customer's tab to open their details.

Tip: Select the arrow next to any status name to jump between Appointments, Waiting, and In Progress.

Waiting screen

How long each guest has been waiting and a summary of the visit reason. Select a customer's tab to open their details.

In-progress screen

Every guest being helped, the time their session started, the rep helping them, and a summary of the visit reason.

03

Checking In a Guest

Existing Xfinity member

Find the account, tell the next rep how to spot the guest and why they're here, then add them to the queue.

  1. Select the person icon, then Xfinity customer (shown as Xfinity Member) or Comcast Business customer. Search by the member's phone number, account number, email, barcode, or QR code.

  2. The addresses tied to that number appear. Select the matching address and the account user.

  3. Add identifiers from the list so the rep assigned to help can easily spot the guest.

  4. Add the reason for the visit:

    • The service they have questions about.
    • Any additional information that will help the assisting rep.
    • Their preferred language.

  5. Confirm the current email and phone number are correct for notifications. Edit them if needed.

  6. Select Add to queue. The member is checked in and added to the Waiting queue. Next, start a session.

Prospective customer

Check serviceability first. That check is also what turns on Acquisition Insights (section 5).

  1. Select the person icon and Xfinity prospect. Enter the customer's name and address to see whether they're in a serviceable area.

  2. Read the serviceability result.

    • Serviceable — continue to step 3.
    • Not serviceable — let the customer know they can go to xfinity.com/mytown and complete the form to hear when Xfinity services reach their area.
    If the address is unknown, enter "Address not available" in the Guest's address field.

  3. Add identifiers so the assisting rep can spot the guest (same list as for members).

  4. Add the reason for the visit: the service they're asking about, extra notes for the rep, and preferred language.

  5. Add the customer's contact information.

  6. Select Add to queue. The customer is checked in and added to the Waiting queue. Next, start a session.

Comcast Business customer

  1. Select the person icon and Comcast Business customer (shown as CB customer). Search by the customer's phone number, account number, or email.

  2. Finish the check-in using the same steps as an existing member.

04

Creating an Appointment

Booking a future visit

The same information as a check-in, plus a date and time.

  1. Select the calendar icon, then choose Xfinity prospect, Xfinity customer, or Comcast Business customer and enter the information as you would for a check-in.

    • Xfinity prospect: enter the customer's name and address.
    • Xfinity customer or Comcast Business customer: enter the phone number attached to their account.

  2. Complete the remaining information:

    • Preferred consultant, if the guest has one.
    • Topics the customer would like to discuss.
    • Additional notes for the assisting rep.
    • Preferred language.

  3. Select an appointment date and time that works best for the customer.

  4. Add the customer's phone number or email so they get updates about the appointment, then select Create appointment.

  5. The new appointment appears under Calendar.

05

Reviewing Customer Details

What the details screen shows

Read this before you walk over. It tells you who you're looking for, why they came in, and what's on their account.

Check-in guests

  • Name and how long they've been waiting.
  • Identifiers to find them in the store.
  • Visit details from check-in: visit reason and services to discuss.
  • Serviceability (new customers) or current account information (existing members).

Appointment guests

  • Name.
  • Appointment date and time.
  • Visit details from booking: reason for visit and services to discuss.
  • Serviceability (new customers) or current account information (existing members).

Other details

  • Contact information from notification preferences, and preferred language.
  • For an existing member, attach the account with their phone number to see:
    • Account type: employee, active, or inactive.
    • Active services.
    • Tenure and Xfinity membership status.
    • The current address on the account.
    • Recent engagement — additions or changes made in the last 30 days.
    • Latest notes — in-store, chat, and call-center interactions from the last year.

AI-powered interactions: Xfinity members

After you check in a member, a Recommended actions banner may show an Account Review Opportunity.

  • It appears only for members who have AI-powered interaction deals available. Not every member will.
  • When you see it, swivel to CSG to explore personalized deals that may give the member better value for their needs.
  • For the deal details, see AI-powered interaction deals available in CSG in Celestial Knowledge.

Celestial Acquisition Insights: prospective customers

AI-powered insights on each prospect who walks in: what they might want, what competitors offer, and personalized recommendations. They help you start a conversation that feels personal, offer the right product faster, and close with less guesswork.

  1. Complete the serviceability check during prospect check-in (section 3, step 1). Insights only surface after it.

  2. For serviceable customers, insights appear at the bottom of the customer details screen. Use them alongside your T-Charts to steer the conversation toward the right solution.

Location recommendations
Key household persona details: who the customer may be and what matters most to them.
Likely to buy
The Xfinity deal a household like this most often chooses, suggested by AI from the persona data.
Please consider
Recommendations by line of business, drawn from what's known about the household, to keep in mind through the conversation.
Edge out the competition
The top ways Xfinity wins against competitors in the household's area, to anticipate objections and sharpen your pitch.
Keep doing discoveryInsights don't replace discovery questions or exploring the customer's needs. They're one more tool for finding the best option.
Insights won't appear when: the customer is out of footprint, they're a Comcast Business customer, or there's already an active account at the address.

07

Know This First

What Point of Sale is for

Sales and returns for in-store merchandise, bill payments, and the daily open and close.

Use it for
  • Selling and returning accessories / in-store merchandise
  • Taking bill payments for customers
  • Opening and closing the store
  • Funding and settling the drawer
Not for
  • Selling Xfinity services
  • Any equipment that has to be activated — for example NOW Internet, prepaid devices or Xfinity Cameras

Before you ring anything

It works the same on the desktop and the iPad, with one exception (checks). Five things to keep straight:

  • Open it from Store Space

    Point of Sale launches straight from the guest check-in — no swiveling to a separate app.

  • Opening & closing

    At open, only the registers need to be opened — iPads don't. At close, any suspended transaction is cancelled automatically, so finish anything you've parked before closing.

  • Exchanges are two transactions

    Do the return first, then ring the new item as its own sale.

  • Checks = iPad only

    Check payments can only be taken on an iPad.

  • Commission follows the login

    The sale is credited to whoever is logged in when it's completed. Make sure it's you.

Completing a Sale

Step by step

Five steps from scan to receipt.

  1. Tap Sale. Tap Sale, then scan the product or type in its SKU.

  2. Build the cart. Keep adding items with Scan item, Look up item or Quick sale. When the cart is complete, tap Checkout.

  3. Choose how they're paying. Select the payment method, then tap Process payment.

    • If the guest's account was attached at check-in, their details show here and can be edited.
    • No account attached? You can still look it up before you process the payment.

  4. Take the payment. Run the card or take cash through RetailPay like you normally do. A Processing card payment overlay shows while it goes through — don't close it.

  5. Send the receipt. Choose Email receipt or Print receipt. If an account is attached, the email address fills in automatically — you can change it.

Good to know

  • Items can be scanned, or manually enter the product SKU.
  • Once in the cart, you can also add items via quick sale or by scanning more items.
  • During checkout, guest details will appear if a customer's account was connected in Celestial Store Space during check-in.
    • Customer details can be edited.
    • If a customer's account was not connected, you can complete a lookup before processing the payment.
  • When accepting cash payments, once you select a printer to complete the transaction it will prepopulate the same printer option for a receipt.
Use the "Skip to specific menu items, functions, or reports" search bar at the top to jump straight to a function instead of hunting for the tile.
11

Completing a Return

Step by step

Doing an exchange? Run this return first, then ring the replacement as a new sale.

  1. Find the original sale. Tap Return and scan the receipt — or find the sale in Transaction history.

  2. Pick the items and where they go. Check each item the customer is returning, choose its inventory destination from the drop-down, then tap Submit.

  3. Review the return. Make sure the right items and the refund amount are correct, then tap Select tender.

    Heads upThe Return comments box on this screen is required.

  4. Refund it. Choose the refund method, then tap Process refund.

  5. Send the receipt. Email or print the customer's receipt.

Good to know

  • If a customer would like to do an exchange, complete a return of the original item first. Then select Back to home to complete a sale of the new item the customer would like to purchase.
  • Credit card returns may take 5–7 business days to be returned, based on their financial institution.

Open Store

Opening the cash drawer

  1. The store's current status will show Store closed. To open the store, click the Open store tile.

  2. Confirm open.

  3. Review store open details and any comments from the previous night's closing, then select Okay.

  4. The store will now show as Store open.

Fund drawer

Load the starting funds into the drawer for the business day.

  1. Select Till.

  2. Select Fund drawer.

  3. If on iPad, select the printer you want to open. Desktop will default to the attached cash drawer.

  4. Enter the amount, ensuring it is equal to the system expectation, then select Add funds.

  5. Verify the amount is equal and continue. You will be taken back to the Till main menu.

If the amount is not equal, provide comments to explain the discrepancy before continuing.
14

Close Store

Settle drawer

Close out the drawer at the end of the business day.

  1. Select Till.

  2. Select Settle drawer.

  3. If on iPad, select the printer you want to settle. Desktop will default to the attached cash drawer.

  4. Select the type of tender you want to declare.

Settle drawer — tender count

  1. Enter the amount for each applicable tender. After entering amounts, select Declare tender to be taken back to the tender count page, then select the next type of tender you want to declare.

  2. When you are done entering all tenders, select Submit count.

  3. Verify all tenders are equal and continue.

  4. Select or scan the printer to print the receipt. You will be taken back to the Back Office Homepage.

If any amount is not equal, provide comments to explain the discrepancy and continue. You will then be prompted to accept the deposit amount or recount the drawer.

Closing the cash drawer

  1. The store's current status will show Store open. To close the store, click the Close store tile.

  2. You must settle all cash drawers that were open before proceeding.

  3. Provide any closing comments needed for the opener, then select Close store.

  4. You will then be taken into the tender count flow to declare tender for all cash drawer deposits. Once all tender is confirmed you will be taken to the Back Office Homepage and the store status will show Store closed.

Heads upClosing the store ends the business day and cancels any suspended transactions. Make sure nothing is left suspended before you close.

At a glance

What
Celestial Customer Space centralizes member information and interactions in one place. You can manage a member's account and give personalized service, with Mercury Assistant at your side.
When
Rolling launch beginning July 7.
Rep impact
The Retail 360 functionality you already know moves into the same tool you use for Point of Sale, Check-in (Store Space), Buy Anywhere Pickup In-Store (BAPIS) and knowledge, plus new capabilities with Mercury Assistant.

What is Customer Space

One screen for the member's account, tools and knowledge, so you can help without swiveling between systems.

Celestial Customer Space is a centralized tool for viewing and managing a member's account. It combines account details, tools, and knowledge in one place. It replaces Retail 360, bringing that functionality into the Celestial platform along with new capabilities like Mercury Assistant.

Benefits to you

  • All customer information in one place for a personalized experience.
  • Less time swiveling between tools.
  • Quick summaries and answers surfaced during the interaction.
  • Faster sales, service, and troubleshooting conversations.

Accessing Customer Space

Start with check-in

Every customer account is reached through check-in in Store Space.

To access any customer account, you must complete check-in in Store Space. Check-in captures key details like customer type, visit reason, and contact information. It does more than manage the queue:

  • Identifies quick interactions, like bill payments or exchanges.
  • Gives you insight into the customer's needs before the interaction starts.
  • Surfaces opportunities for sales, upgrades, or support.
Completing check-in sets you up to start the interaction prepared and confident.

Quick account lookup

For account reviews, research, and quick hits, like equipment returns on a busy day.

  1. Select the Store drop-down, then select Customer lookup.

  2. Search by account number, phone number, customer name, address, or email.

Prospective customer (new sale)

Starting a new sale from the queue

The path for a guest who isn't an Xfinity member yet.

  1. In Store Space, select the prospective customer's name from the waiting queue, then select Start.

  2. Select your sale path: XOE (new customer) or NOW.

  3. You'll be asked whether the customer wants Xfinity Mobile.

    • Yes: ID authentication is triggered before you continue to XOE.
    • No: continue to the XOE plan builder. If they change their mind later in the interaction, you can add mobile later through XM360.

  4. Begin building the order as normal in XOE.

  5. After the sale is complete, you'll return to Store Space with Point of Sale and Customer Space navigation buttons available for the session.

Mercury Assistant

What Mercury Assistant does

An AI-powered panel built into Customer Space to help you get answers and take action faster.

  • Quick summaries of the customer's account.
  • Suggested next steps based on the visit.
  • Access to Celestial knowledge through Ask Me Anything (AMA).

Agentic tasks

One-tap buttons that save you time with summaries built from the member's account:

  • Recent bill summary
  • Summary of interaction
  • Summary of service issues
  • Summary of credits

Recommended tasks

Suggested actions based on the visit reason noted in Store Space. Examples you may see:

  • Explain my bill
  • Explain Xfinity Mobile due date
  • Add Xfinity Internet service
  • Add Xfinity Mobile service

Ask Me Anything (AMA)

AMA finds answers to product, policy, or tool questions. It gives knowledge-based answers so you don't have to swivel to Celestial Knowledge, but it won't pull customer-specific account answers.

For best practices when searching in Mercury Assistant, look up Celestial tips: AMA and search in Celestial Knowledge.

Customer Space FAQs

Common questions

Can I use Mercury Assistant for a prospective customer?
Customer Space features, including Mercury Assistant, are only available once a customer has pending services. For a prospective customer, use the information shown in Store Space from the check-in or appointment, and swivel to Celestial Knowledge if you need knowledge information.
Can I type my AMA question in Spanish?
Yes. If you type your question in Spanish, AMA will interpret it and respond in Spanish.
Can Mercury Assistant give me information about an account?
No. Outside of the agentic tasks, questions about a specific customer's account get a generic answer sourced from Celestial Knowledge. Use AMA the way you'd use Celestial Knowledge search.
Does this change how I use XM360, XOE, and other tools?
No. All tool processes are business as usual. Use Customer Space as a launch point to get to the tools you need quickly. You still need to swivel to CSG for any core service changes.
How do I report issues with Customer Space?
Submit issues through TechSpot using the Care Application Issue form. Fill in all applicable fields completely and accurately, and include any details or screenshots that help.
